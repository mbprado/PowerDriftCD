# Phase 1B: controlled steering response in Stage 1 — 2026-10-20

**Question / hypothesis:** does changing only the arcade steering input during the repeatable Stage 1 interval produce a repeatable, visible divergence from the centered replay? **Baseline:** the [fixed-input Stage 1 replay](2026-10-20-arcade-stage1-input-replay.md) reached the race with identical fresh-run screenshots. **Acceptance:** same images before the pulse, different images afterward, two matching pulse runs, and no invented internal camera variables. **Result — PASS, verified by execution:** the fresh centered and two steering-pulse runs matched at callbacks 300, 600 and 900. Both pulse runs then matched each other exactly at every sampled callback from 1020 to 1800, while differing from the centered control.

## Controlled change

**Verified by execution:** the original [`tools/phase1b_mame_input.lua`](../../tools/phase1b_mame_input.lua) now accepts optional `PDRIFT_STEER_PULSE`. With no value it retains the prior centered input schedule. With `PDRIFT_STEER_PULSE=192`, it changes the MAME `:ADC.5` `Paddle` field from 128 to 192 at callback 960 and restores 128 at callback 1080. Coin, start, pedal and all other scripted inputs are identical. The script also captures callbacks 1020, 1080 and 1140 between the prior standard checkpoints. Its tested SHA-256 was `52b7a58e2b53c07ea74d70188dda0b41be75e04fbb04653bfd681e0cb430b509`. Value 192 is within the field's enumerated 32–224 range; its exact real-cabinet steering angle is **unsupported**.

**Image interpretation — verified by execution:** the callback-1020 centered image shows the car behind a straight road section; the pulse image shows the car near the outer road edge and a sharply tilted rendered view. At callback 1200, the car position, road view and HUD placement/rank differ. A private raster comparison found 55,415 of 71,680 pixels different at callback 1020. These observations establish a repeatable input-to-image response, not a numerical camera banking angle or a track-coordinate difference. The after-pulse images remain different because game state evolved differently; a later match was not expected or claimed.

**Limitations / next test — hypothesis:** no RAM field, track segment, car position or camera transform has been identified. The [MAME 0.289 Y-Board driver](https://github.com/mamedev/mame/blob/mame0289/src/mame/sega/segaybd.cpp) maps main-CPU work RAM at `$1F0000–$1FFFFF` and shared RAM at `$0C0000–$0CFFFF`; this is documented emulator source, not a discovered game-variable map. Next capture a bounded, private RAM window at the same callback in centered and pulse runs, compare within-condition repeats before flagging candidate changes, and correlate any candidates with debugger write traces. Do not label a changed address “camera,” “curvature” or “physics” until independent evidence supports it. No REA/Ghidra analysis or Sega CD camera-interface change was performed.
