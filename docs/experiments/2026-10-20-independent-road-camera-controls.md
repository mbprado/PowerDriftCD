# 2026-10-20 independent road camera controls

**Question / hypothesis:** can the sub-CPU evolve curvature, ridge, bank and horizon on separate schedules, without per-state camera parameter bytes, and still meet the 30 Hz deadline for the 64×64 ASIC road? **Baseline — verified by execution:** the [phase-indexed camera](2026-10-20-phase-indexed-road-camera.md) completed three 60-update U.S. Model 2 runs at 30 Hz in BlastEm, with worst image-ready time 509 emulated stopwatch ticks after the previous VBlank. **Acceptance evidence:** 60 checked ASIC outputs, 59 successive two-VBlank intervals within 1,085–1,088 ticks, no upload misses, and three reproducible cold boots.

**Result — verified by execution:** all three U.S. Model 2 runs completed 60 checked outputs and produced identical frame records. The VBlank interval was 1,086–1,087 ticks, with zero cadence or upload misses. The same private disc reached `GVOK` and update 60 under the owner-labelled U.S. Model 1 BIOS. This is BlastEm L1 evidence, not a physical-hardware result.

## Method and correctness

**Verified by execution:** the sub-CPU decodes frame number 0–59 and calculates four separate controls: curve phase `2 × frame mod 96`, ridge phase `5 × frame mod 192`, bank `floor(triangle(7 × frame mod 24) / 3) − 2`, and horizon `1 + floor(triangle(11 × frame mod 24) / 2)`, where `triangle(p) = min(p, 24 − p)`. Texture phase remains `2 × (frame mod 24)`. Curve and ridge samples come from the same shared 96-byte and 192-byte sine tables as before. Bank spans −2…2 and horizon 1…7 in the 60-frame sequence. The sub-CPU builds all 64 trace rows, triggers the ASIC, and returns 2M Word RAM; the main CPU checks the additive output-word sum, stages 2,048 bytes, and uploads them by VDP DMA.

**Correctness limit — verified by execution:** all 60 expected sums were generated from the independent-control host reference, and all 60 ASIC results passed their in-program checks. All 60 images differ from the prior phase-indexed scene; 51,235 of 61,440 output words differ in an offline comparison. The separate controls therefore exercise a changed image sequence, not a pixel-identical A/B timing control. **Unsupported:** the additive sum cannot exclude compensating pixel errors, and no per-frame VRAM dump was taken. This experiment does not yet accept arbitrary camera inputs from gameplay; its independently evolving controls are deterministic functions of frame number.

## Engineering decision

**Hypothesis:** the frame-derived independent controls are viable for this small synthetic scene in BlastEm, but the 32-tick observed margin is too small to assume additional scenery, a larger viewport or gameplay processing will fit. The next experiment should stress output area and/or introduce a live camera-command interface while keeping these controls independently adjustable. Preserve the existing 30 Hz measured milestone before changing the transfer path; if deadlines slip, isolate trace preparation, ASIC time and VDP staging rather than reducing visual fidelity without evidence. The 60 Hz target, arbitrary camera movement, stock-hardware performance and a complete road renderer remain **unsupported**.
