# 2026-10-20 phase-indexed road camera

**Question / hypothesis:** can the sub-CPU derive the 64 per-line curvature and ridge samples from a camera phase and two shared lookup tables, instead of reading 64 precomputed pairs for every camera state, while retaining the 64×64 road's 30 Hz update cadence? **Baseline — verified by execution:** the [compact runtime-trace experiment](2026-10-20-runtime-road-traces.md) completed three U.S. Model 2 runs at 30 Hz. Its worst image-ready time was 501 emulated stopwatch ticks after the previous VBlank, leaving about 42 ticks before the next VBlank. **Expected evidence:** 60 checked ASIC images per run, the prescribed two-VBlank cadence, no missed VBlank uploads, and a separately measured trace-construction window.

**Result — verified by execution:** three independent cold boots of the new private image on BlastEm 1.0.0 with the owner-labelled U.S. Model 2 BIOS completed all 60 checked ASIC outputs. Every run had 59 successive VBlank intervals of 1,086–1,087 stopwatch ticks, with zero cadence or upload misses. The same image reached its completion marker on the owner-labelled U.S. Model 1 BIOS. This is L1 emulator evidence; physical hardware was not tested.

## Camera method and correctness

**Verified by execution:** the sub-CPU reads 48 bytes of bank/horizon parameters for 24 phases and two signed sample tables of 96 and 192 bytes. It derives the curve index from `4 × phase + scanline`, and the ridge index from `8 × phase + 3 × scanline`, wrapping inside each table. It computes depth, scale band, source starts and steps for each of 64 trace rows and passes the trace to the Sega CD graphics ASIC. The main CPU verifies the additive output-word checksum and stages the 2,048-byte 64×64 image in main RAM for VDP DMA. The total camera data is 336 bytes, versus 3,168 bytes of prior compact per-state samples and 12,288 bytes of complete precomputed traces. These sizes exclude shared stamp graphics, program code, and expected checksums.

**Correctness limit — verified by execution:** this is a new, quantized lookup-table scene, not byte-identical output to the preceding compact-state scene. Offline comparison found changed values in 20 of 1,536 trace rows over the 24 phases; 16 phase images changed, totalling 224 different 16-bit output words. The new expected checksums were generated from the new trace equations, and all 60 ASIC outputs passed their checks in the running image. Its *final* screenshot had the same SHA-256 as the prior baseline screenshot, which does not imply every intermediate frame matched. An additive checksum can miss compensating pixel errors; there is no independent per-frame VRAM dump in this run.

**Timing interpretation — verified by execution:** these are 12-bit Sega CD stopwatch observations as emulated by BlastEm, with each measured window shorter than the 4,096-tick wrap. Compared with the compact runtime baseline, median trace construction increased from 104 to 112 ticks and worst image-ready time from 501 to 509 ticks. The worst observed ready margin before the next approximately 543-tick VBlank boundary is about 34 ticks. The 167–168-tick staging window includes both checksum verification and copying; ownership/setup/ASIC/return is a composite window. The ASIC trigger-to-busy-clear window was 96/97/97 ticks. Main/sub-CPU cycle counts and stock-hardware timings are **unsupported** by this experiment. The 34-tick margin is too narrow to extrapolate to a larger viewport or gameplay load.

## Decision and next test

**Hypothesis:** a phase-indexed camera with shared tables is a plausible sub-CPU workload for this small synthetic scene, but the remaining ready margin makes a broader 64×64 scene risky. The 24 fixed phase states, bank/horizon bytes, lookup tables, stamps, and output checksums remain precomputed. Truly independent camera controls, scenery, larger viewport, and 60 Hz have not been measured. Next, vary curvature, ridge and bank independently at runtime and determine whether the scene remains within the VBlank deadline; first preserve 30 Hz and checksum-checked output. If it misses, isolate the added arithmetic and test smaller regions or less frequent updates. No production renderer or physical-hardware test is implied.

**Visual preview — verified by execution:** `sh scripts/build-perspective-road.sh build/private/us-m1-startup-prefix.bin compare` makes a private image that cycles three 24-state loops labelled `PRE`, then three labelled `RUNTIME`, and repeats for quick viewing. One full 144-update cycle was observed in Model 2 BlastEm with both labels and a visible road; [PRE](data/perspective-road-compare-pre.png) and [RUNTIME](data/perspective-road-compare-runtime.png) captures are at different camera phases. A later capture near the mode switch did not establish matching scene output, so this preview is not performance or pixel-equivalence evidence. No further comparison runs are required for this roadmap step.
