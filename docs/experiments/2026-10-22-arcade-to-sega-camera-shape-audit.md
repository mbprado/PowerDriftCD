# Phase 1B: conditional arcade-to-Sega CD camera-shape audit — 2026-10-22

**Question / hypothesis:** can the current five-word Sega CD camera packet produce the *lateral multi-depth shape* of a short, matched-position arcade steering response? **Baseline:** previously captured centered/left/right MAME projection words at three fixed ROM depths, plus the existing 128×64 Sega CD host trace builder. **Method:** assume two explicit depth-to-scanline correspondences, derive every distinct curve-phase/bank lateral profile allowed by the tested packet ranges, fit one common coordinate gain on two epochs, and inverse-fit the third as a held-out calibration check. **Expected evidence:** exact agreement of the reduced Sega model with the project's host trace builder, no use of unmatched arcade positions, a fixed gain for the held-out epoch, separate flat-shift control, and explicit error units. **Result — INCONCLUSIVE for interface sufficiency, verified by execution of the offline analyzer:** the current synthetic curve/bank waveform gives only a modest fit under one assumed mapping and a worse-than-flat fit under the other. Neither mapping or coordinate gain is independently calibrated, and no real Sega CD frame was rendered from arcade data.

It confirms identical upstream lookup-position words across all three steering conditions at projection callbacks 960, 964, 968, and 972. For each callback `f`, this audit reads the *new* projected X from callback `f+2` at depths `$3600`, `$3800`, `$3A00` and subtracts the centered value. Callback 960 yields six zero differences and serves as an alignment control. The source geometry tuple at each selected depth is invariant across the three steering runs. The underlying records are owner-local, ignored files.

**Verified by execution of source inspection and 360 host-reference comparisons:** at a fixed output row, the current 128×64 trace builder's center source-X is `136 + curve_sine[(curve_phase + row) mod 96] + floor(bank × (row − 32) / 8)`. The analyzer checks this reduced term against `live_camera_lines()` for all 60 existing packets at three rows in each of two mappings: 180 exact comparisons per mapping. The tested packet range is curve phase 0–95 and signed bank −2…2. The other three packet words affect source Y, depth band/scale, or texture offset in this narrow center-X calculation; they can still affect **visible pixels**. The reduced model is not a complete road-edge or ASIC image model.

**Hypothesis, explicitly uncalibrated:** ROM depths `$3600/$3800/$3A00` correspond respectively to output rows `(56,40,24)` or `(48,32,16)` from near to far. The analyzer assumes a positive common gain in eighth-step increments from 0.125 to 32 arcade projected-X words per Sega source-X pixel. It chooses gain using callback 964 and 968 left/right data, then holds it fixed at callback 972. At every epoch it may choose a separate center packet and separate left/right packets, while left and right must share that epoch's center packet. This is an **optimistic inverse fit**, including at the held-out epoch: the held-out target is used to choose packets. It tests an upper bound on representation under each assumed mapping, **not** a predictive upstream-to-packet adapter. No temporal smoothness or CPU cost is imposed.

## Fit result

**Verified by execution of the offline analyzer:** errors below are root-mean-square residuals over the selected projected-X differences, in **arcade projected-X words**. The flat-shift control uses one constant lateral displacement per steering side and epoch, fitted from all three depths. Its combined training RMSE is approximately 2.44 words and its callback-972 RMSE is 7.20 words.

**Verified by execution:** at callback 972 the arcade left-minus-center X profile was `(37,27,20)` and right-minus-center `(−37,−26,−19)` at near/mid/far depth. For the first assumed map, the best available left profile after applying the fixed gain was `(29.25,34.125,9.75)`; the best right profile was `(−29.25,−29.25,−14.625)`. Their residuals are structured across depth, not just a uniform offset. The second assumed map performed worse. The fitted packet tuples and all six residuals per epoch remain in ignored `build/private/phase1b-camera-shape-audit.json`, SHA-256 `f33d8ed6069e63fc3a1b026b20ec32796ecc9efd1874d75c62130ce41d637cd1`.

**Inference from verified execution:** the *specific* synthetic sine-table and ±2 bank-control ranges in the working five-word implementation are not a convincing numerical description of this arcade projection signal under either tested correspondence. This is not evidence that five communication words are intrinsically insufficient: a different shared curve table, depth lookup, packet interpretation, or correct arcade-to-screen mapping could change the result. The flat-shift control's similar or lower error reinforces the need for calibration before designing new control dimensions.
