## Source intervention and result

**Verified by execution in isolated MAME 0.289:** linked-list inspection showed active Y-sprite pages advancing `$8`, `$E`, `$C`, `$A` at callbacks 978–981. A callback-980 page-`$A` hide changed both detected edges on screen 981. Seven descriptor slots were plausible at the mapped native edge coordinates; individual hides identified `$A4A0` (left Y=157), `$A5D0` (right Y=157–163 and left Y=158), and `$A620` (left Y=159–163) as the three-slot set. The combined private intervention changed exactly the hide bytes at those three source-RAM offsets. The after-write dump matched the override byte for byte.

**Verified by execution:** three fresh replays with that same override produced identical native-frame-980 Y bitmaps and decoded screen-981 RGB. All captured screens 960–980 matched the fresh native baseline. The fresh baseline matched the previous baseline for screens 960–982 and the frame-980 Y bitmap. The 16B layer was clear at all 14 detected edge pixels. Mapping each edge through the captured pre-rotation transform found an opaque native Y-bitmap word before the intervention and `$FFFF` transparency afterward; every corresponding final RGB pixel changed. The private [audit](../../tools/phase1b_screen981_source_audit.py) checks these facts and reports source attribution `PASS`. This proves those sampled displayed pixels depend on the three descriptors; it does **not** prove every detected edge is a pure road boundary.

**Verified by execution of the fixed host comparison:** the [case file](data/phase1b-screen981-exploratory-case.json) compared screen 981 with the existing screen-975 original-art host preview. Positive deltas mean the host edge lies farther right or its width is larger than the calibrated arcade target.
