## Writer, arithmetic and result

**Verified by execution:** a focused pulse-200 main-CPU write watch found exactly 20 `$0C0864` writes, all at post-write PC `$0344A0`. The first 11 values stayed `$00FE`; the remaining nine decreased by three. Private instruction inspection identifies `$034478–$034490` as a signed 16-bit bounded update of global `$0CEA12`: subtract old global from desired `D0`, clamp to ±`D1`, add to the global word, then copy the result to `A5+$24` at `$03449C`. The [Python predictor](../../tools/phase1b_state24_predict.py) reproduced all **20/20** existing pulse-200 development writes from input-only `D0`, `D1` and old `$0CEA12` records at `$034478`.

**Verified by execution:** the pulse-188 input-only replay yielded 20 earlier operand sets. The model froze 20 `$24` predictions in private JSON SHA-256 `422b3553b6c2e4158b80c6943a09978652381d6758ba316fac3aec19f45c13fc` **before** a separate target-only MAME replay. Held-out comparison matched **20/20** exactly, with no numerical or callback-marker difference. Desired `D0` was `$00FE` on 12 updates and `$009E` on eight; `D1=3` throughout. The output remained `$00FE` for 12 updates, then decreased by three on eight updates to `$00E6`. Two additional independent target replays produced byte-identical logs. A separate PC-only replay saw `$0344A0 → $034D44 → $034E24` once at each of the 20 markers, so this `$24` write preceded the validated `$86` and `$7C` consumers on the exercised main-CPU path. Callback markers are not measured video frames or original hardware cycles.

**Limitation:** desired `D0`, limit `D1` and old `$0CEA12` were observed before the bounded update. This result explains how `$24` is prepared and timed; it does **not** predict the desired operand or the field from raw steering/track state alone. The held-out schedule exercised a positive step limit of three, a zero delta and a negative clamp, but not positive movement, a larger limit, or the conditional paths before `$034478`. Calling `$24` a physical speed or camera coordinate remains **unsupported**.
