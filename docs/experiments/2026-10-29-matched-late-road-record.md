# Phase 1B: matched late road record across steering schedules — 2026-10-29

## Question and method

**Hypothesis:** the two visible `$E5D0` descriptors in the [dual-schedule test](2026-10-29-dual-schedule-roadslot.md) are temporary sprite-list allocations of different source records. A useful comparison should follow one earlier geometry object across both schedules, even if its output slot changes. **Baseline:** pulse-204 and pulse-56 callback-950 replays, their captured geometry rows and shared lists, and the validated callback-991 → native frame-990 → composed screen-992 timing. **Acceptance:** identify one ROM-backed geometry record with the same source sample in both schedules; verify all eight main-CPU shared-list writes, the sub-CPU load and output slot, and a visible exact-slot hide in each schedule. Repeat a newly identified exact-slot hide in three fresh MAME runs. This experiment does not accept a generic image difference as a camera prediction.

## Result

**Verified by execution — PASS for a matched source record:** object record `$0C1D00` uses the same captured geometry table `$01D3E8` and source words `$20BF/$0790` in both schedules at callback 989. Its projected raw word pair differs: `$FF07/$0039` for pulse 204 and `$013E/$0038` for pulse 56. These are arcade fixed-point fields; no physical coordinate units are assigned here. The complete 16-byte projected record appears exactly once at shared-list offset `$0240` in each schedule's callback-988–991 captures. Main-CPU PC `$033E8E` wrote all eight shared-list words at `$0C9A40–$0C9A4E` with `A5=$0C1D00` in both runs. The sub-CPU read the same record class at PC `$00DD6A`, but used different output pointers.

| Schedule | Shared-list first longword | Shared-list offset | Sub-CPU output slot | Visible page-`$E` slot | Graphic word | Screen-992 changed region |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pulse 204 | `$8B7C0020` | `$0240` | `$C5D0` | `$E5D0` | `$28B9` | X=0–212, Y=176–223 |
| Pulse 56 | `$8B740020` | `$0240` | `$C5F0` | `$E5F0` | `$2898` | X=187–319, Y=185–223 |

**Verified by execution:** the pulse-204 `$E5D0` one-byte hide was already repeated three times. A new callback-991 pulse-56 `$E5F0` one-byte hide changed **10,421 of 262,144 native 16-bit words** at MAME frame 990 and **2,814 of 71,680 composed RGB pixels** on Lua screenshot 992. Other captured native frames 988–995 and composed screens 960–1000 were unchanged. Two further fresh MAME runs reproduced the same post-write RAM, native bitmap and composed-screen checksums. The prior pulse-56 `$E5D0` attribution instead traces to *another* object record, `$0C1D40`, at shared offset `$0260`; matching by sprite slot alone had paired different geometry samples.

**Verified by execution:** the [machine-readable audit](../../tools/phase1b_matched_late_record_audit.py) checks the geometry row, the unique full shared-list tuple on four callbacks, eight contiguous main-CPU writes, the sub-CPU load and output pointer, one changed descriptor byte, exact affected frames and three-run output equality for both schedules. Its result gives a reproducible correspondence between one **ROM-backed source sample** and two visible arcade outputs. **Unsupported:** that `$0C1D00` represents a specific physical track distance or that the screen-region displacement is caused by the predicted `$0CEA00` phase alone. Steering also changes other camera and scene state, and the two screenshots have different occlusion and sprite content.

**Decision:** use the matched object and ROM sample as the first bounded late-window cross-schedule geometry landmark. Do not infer a screen-space projection rule from the intervention-mask bounds, fit a Sega CD five-word packet to them, or alter the renderer. The source record and projection chain can now be investigated without relying on temporary sprite addresses.
