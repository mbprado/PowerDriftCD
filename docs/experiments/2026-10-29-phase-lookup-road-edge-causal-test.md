# Phase 1B: position lookup offset to visible road edge — 2026-10-29

## Question and controlled test

**Hypothesis:** the newly predicted `$0CEA00` phase affects visible road geometry through its derived `$0CEA04` lookup offset. **Baseline:** local `4dd7cec`, pulse 204 from the owner-private callback-950 state, isolated MAME 0.289, and the [source-to-phase prediction](2026-10-29-state24-to-position-phase-heldout.md). **Acceptance:** a temporary offset change must alter the generated-table index, projected road data, shared list, previously attributed `$E630/$C630` descriptors, and unoccluded left-edge pixels. A zero-delta control and exact-slot hides must distinguish a road contribution from a generic screen difference.

**Verified by execution:** the [capture script](../../tools/phase1b_mame_input.lua) armed two main-CPU debugger breakpoints for one update. Immediately after `$034548` wrote `$0CEA04`, the entry breakpoint at `$03454E` added a specified *byte offset*. The exit breakpoint at `$034B02`, after the generated-RAM table read, logged `D1` and restored the exact old `$0CEA04`. `$0CEA02` and `$0CEA00` were not modified. The intervention therefore tests a **derived lookup dependency**, not a naturally occurring camera-phase change. The zero-delta control used the same breakpoints and capture settings.

## Result and timing

**Verified by execution, small-value control:** at callback 963, adding `$0020` changed the table index `$0026 → $0025`, but geometry CSV, Y-sprite source snapshots and all 23 screens 960–982 were byte-identical to the zero-delta control. This shows that an index change alone need not cause a visible difference at this point.

**Verified by execution, later-frame control:** at callback 963, adding `$01C0` selected index `$0011`; eight projected rows, other Y-sprite descriptors and screens 967–971 changed. The previously attributed `$E630` and `$C630` descriptors and sampled screen-967/968 road bounds stayed unchanged. Thus the effect did not reach those particular road pixels from that update.

**Verified by execution — PASS for a bounded causal path:** at callback **961**, the same temporary `$01C0` change moved `$0CEA04` from `$02F4` to `$04B4`, selected generated-table index `$001B` instead of `$0025`, then restored `$02F4`. The corresponding private ROM coefficient words are `$0054` versus `$0073`. The following captured globals at callback 962 changed `$0C009C`-related X from `$E808` to `$E812` and Y from `$FB23` to `$FB25`. Eight sampled projection rows changed by callback 964. The shared list was identical at callback 963 and differed at callback 964. Screens 960–964 were identical; screens 965–969 changed. These screen differences are counts of decoded RGB pixels, not emulated timing measurements.

**Verified by execution:** Y-sprite `$E630` at callback 966 and `$C630` at callback 967 both changed while their graphic word remained `$3888`. At screen 967, the sampled left road boundary at Y=159–161 moved from X=`66,64,62` to `68,66,64`. At screen 968, Y=159–163 moved from X=`65,64,62,61,60` to `66,66,64,63,62`. Each exact-slot hide changed **all 3/3 and 5/5** left-edge pixels at those new coordinates, respectively, with earlier screens unchanged. Each private override changed only the descriptor's hide byte. This establishes that the shifted pixels still have a visible dependency on the changed road-like Y-sprite descriptors; it does not establish decoded world-space track coordinates.

**Verified by execution:** two additional fresh `$01C0` callback-961 MAME runs reproduced the screen-967 PNG, all 23 screens, geometry CSV and callback-966/967 Y-sprite source snapshots byte-for-byte. The [audit script](../../tools/phase1b_phase_lookup_road_audit.py) checks the controls, breakpoint order and restoration, geometry/list order, repeated captures, exact one-byte hides and pixel samples. It returned `PASS`: eight changed projected rows and eight visible left samples. Its ignored JSON SHA-256 is `d8843b18a79ccb74c70744876f1dc4e60b6b6c8d7daccc1b346940d7a2a2b0e2`.

## Interpretation and next test

**Verified by execution:** the `$0CEA04` table-offset path can causally move the sampled road boundary when the change enters early enough and selects a sufficiently different table value. **Hypothesis:** a naturally evolving phase sequence may carry useful road curvature or camera-motion information. The tested `$01C0` byte-offset jump corresponds to a large phase step and does not represent measured natural steering behavior. Exact arithmetic from the intervened coefficient through every changed projected object and sprite remains incomplete. The right edge and other screen differences were not used as evidence for this specific road path. The five-word Sega CD packet is **INCONCLUSIVE**; no renderer interface change follows from this result.

**Next bounded test — hypothesis:** choose a natural, previously unseen phase/track sequence, predict `$0CEA04` without reading target outputs, and compare one fixed unoccluded left-edge sample under an explicit arcade-to-Sega CD coordinate calibration. Keep descriptor source attribution and timing separate from packet fitting.
